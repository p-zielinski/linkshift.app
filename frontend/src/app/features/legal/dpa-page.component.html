Roles

  • Customer (you) — data controller for end visitors who request your redirect URLs.
  • {{ siteConfig.legalName }} (Operator) — data processor for redirect routing, analytics, and configuration storage on your documented instructions.

Subject matter and duration

Subject matter: redirect routing, aggregated redirect analytics, and storage of your redirect configuration (rules, link maps, domains, and related settings).

Duration: for the term of your Service agreement — from account creation or first use of live redirect traffic until termination of the Service, plus any retention period described in the Privacy Policy or this DPA.

Categories of data subjects and personal data

For live redirect processing under this DPA, the Operator processes the following on your documented instructions:

  • Data subjects: end visitors who request your redirect URLs.
  • Categories of personal data: redirect request metadata (HTTP method, path, query parameters, destination URL, link map keys) and aggregated redirect analytics. Visitor IP addresses are not stored in the analytics database. IP addresses may appear in short-lived operational logs as described in the Privacy Policy.

Customer instructions

You instruct the Operator through configuration in the dashboard and Management API — redirect rules, link maps, domain groups, analytics usage, and related settings. You are responsible for the lawfulness of your configuration, including destinations that use placeholders such as {{ '{' }}ip{{ '}' }}. See Live traffic and visitor privacy for controller responsibilities.

Processor obligations

  • Process personal data only on documented instructions, except where applicable law requires otherwise.
  • Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data (encryption in transit, access controls, monitoring).
  • Ensure personnel with access to personal data are bound by confidentiality.
  • Engage subprocessors listed below under written terms that impose data protection obligations comparable to this DPA.
  • Assist you with data subject requests where feasible, taking into account the nature of processing and information available to the Operator.
  • Upon termination of the Service, delete or return end-visitor personal data per your instruction, except where applicable law requires retention, subject to a reasonable operational wind-down period.

Breach notification

The Operator will notify you without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting your end-visitor data processed under this DPA, with the information required by applicable law.
