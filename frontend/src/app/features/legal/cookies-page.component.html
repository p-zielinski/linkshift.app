Essential cookies

The only first-party cookie we set is refresh_token for session renewal (documented below). Interface state and account cache are stored in browser local or session storage — not in additional first-party cookies.

Authentication cookie

  • refresh_token — HttpOnly; Secure in production; SameSite=Strict; path /api/v1/auth/; used to refresh your session; essential. In production, this cookie typically expires after about 2 hours (configurable via JWT_REFRESH_EXPIRES_IN); development environments may use longer values.

Third-party services (functional)

We load limited third-party scripts for security and checkout. These are functional, not advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

  • Cloudflare Turnstile — bot protection on the documentation assistant.
  • Paddle.js — checkout overlay when upgrading your plan. Paddle may set third-party cookies on Paddle's domains during checkout (for example, payment session and fraud prevention). Paddle.js may also load Profitwell (public.profitwell.com) for subscription analytics as part of checkout. See Paddle's Privacy Policy. These are functional, not advertising.
  • Google Fonts — typography fonts (Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Mono) and Material Icons loaded from Google servers; Google may process your IP address when fonts are fetched.

Local storage and session storage

In addition to cookies, the Service stores limited data in your browser's local storage and session storage. These technologies are essential or functional — we do not use them for advertising or cross-site tracking.

  • Account cache (local storage): signed-in profile and organization details under user_data and organization_data so the dashboard loads faster after refresh. Removed when you sign out.
  • Interface preferences (local storage): dashboard mode (linkshift-dashboard-mode), selected domain group (linkshift-active-domain-group), and onboarding or setup checklist progress (such as dashboard-onboarding-confirmed and linkshift-setup-checklist).
  • Documentation assistant history (local storage): recent chats on your device under linkshift_docs_assistant_history_v1. You can clear it in the assistant UI.
  • OpenAPI spec cache (session storage): a cached copy of the API specification under linkshift.docs.openapi.v1 for the current browser tab session.
  • Documentation Try Me (session storage): an API key (linkshift.docs.tryMe.apiKey) and request drafts (linkshift.docs.tryMe.drafts.v1) for the current browser tab session.

Signing out removes only the account profile cache. Other local and session storage items persist until you clear them in the UI or in your browser settings.

For more detail, see our Privacy Policy (Browser storage).

No advertising cookies

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies.

Managing cookies and browser storage

You can control cookies and clear local or session storage in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies or clearing stored data may prevent the Service from functioning properly.
